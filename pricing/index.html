Pay for what you use

Usage comes from a prepaid wallet. Send fewer messages and you pay less.

These are US and Canada rates. Texts and calls in the UAE and other countries have their own prices, and I confirm the exact rate with you before we start.

Texting setup

Phone numbers usually need a one-time carrier registration before texting works well. I handle this for you.

Prices can change

Online payments carry normal payment processor fees, and platform prices change over time, so I confirm current rates before you start.
