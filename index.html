Questions people ask

What is GoHighLevel? GoHighLevel (GHL) is an all-in-one CRM and marketing automation platform. It combines lead capture, pipelines, calendars and SMS/email automation in one system, so a business doesn't need ten different tools.

Do you build websites as well as automations? Yes. I design and build responsive websites and landing pages, connect their forms to your CRM, and set up the automation that runs after a lead arrives.

Can you run Google or Meta ad campaigns? I can set up campaigns, landing pages, lead forms and conversion tracking. I don't manage large ad budgets, and I won't promise specific results.

Can you guarantee search rankings? No one can honestly guarantee rankings. I set up the foundations: site structure, metadata, technical checks and Search Console. Results depend on many factors, and ongoing SEO is scoped separately.

Do you work with agencies? Yes. I can build websites, landing pages, CRM workflows and integrations for agencies, behind the scenes and under their brand where appropriate. Partnerships can be discussed confidentially.

What does it cost? It depends on scope. Tell me what you need and the budget you have in mind, and I'll suggest a realistic starting point. My pricing is built for small businesses.

What businesses can benefit from automation? Any business that relies on leads and appointments: clinics, real estate, home services, salons, gyms, law firms and more. If people contact you and you need to follow up, automation helps.

Can you build my GoHighLevel CRM? Yes. I can set up your CRM, pipelines and stages so every lead is organized and easy to track.

Can you automate lead follow-up? Yes. I build automated SMS and email sequences so new leads get a quick response and stay engaged until they book.

Can you automate appointment booking? Yes. I set up booking calendars with automated confirmations and reminders, so fewer appointments get missed.

Can you recover missed calls automatically? Yes. A missed call can trigger a text-back and create a lead in your CRM, so a busy phone line doesn't quietly cost you a customer.

Can you connect my existing tools? In most cases, yes. Through APIs, webhooks or tools like n8n and Make, I can connect GoHighLevel to the software you already use.

Can you build custom workflows? Yes. If your business has a process that doesn't fit a standard template, I build a custom automation around it.

Do I need GoHighLevel before contacting you? No. If you don't have GoHighLevel yet, I can help you get set up as part of the project.

How does the consultation work? We start with a free call where I learn about your business and current process. From there, I put together a plan for what to automate and how.
